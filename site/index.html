Q1. How big is the boom, and is it more chips or more money per chip?

Korean memory exports: value vs weight

Value rose roughly fourfold in a year. Weight barely moved.

HS 854232 covers all Korean memory (Samsung too), not just SK hynix. Weight is a weak volume measure: HBM is heavier per chip than commodity DRAM.

Q2. Is the growth durable volume, or price that can reverse?

Where year-on-year growth comes from

2024: price-led, with volume falling. Mid-2025 to 2025 Q4: volume-led (up about 20-25%). Since 2026 Q1: price again, almost entirely.

Unit value is not a price. It also moves with product mix (HBM vs commodity) and customer mix.

Q3. Is price growth slowing? Do two independent sources agree?

3-month momentum: export unit value vs DDR4 contract price

Both have rolled over from their spring highs but are still positive. Slowing, not falling.

Contract series is DDR4 8Gb only, assembled from news reports of DRAMeXchange. It's a legacy product, so it shows the direction of pricing, not SK hynix's mix.

Q4. Can this be read before SK hynix reports?

Nowcast from exports vs reported revenue (KRW tn)

Revenue(q) = last quarter's revenue × growth in Korean memory exports (KRW). Average error 7.4% vs 17% for the best baseline. With only the first month of the quarter the error is 12.5%; with all three, 7.7%. It understated revenue in 10 of 15 quarters.

15 quarters only. China fabs are outside Korean customs. Uses today's customs values, not first releases.

What would change my view: unit-value and contract momentum both turn negative for two consecutive months; weight growth fails to pick up as price growth fades; the nowcast gap to reported revenue flips from under- to over-prediction.
