Implications for the thesis

05Conclusion
Outlook

What the data implies for SK hynix

  1. Earnings are price-driven, and visible early. Korean memory export value is a year ago on weight. SK hynix revenue tracks these exports roughly one-for-one (correlation QoQ, YoY), so customs data reads each quarter about two months in (/ direction calls).
  2. Commodity DRAM has moved from accelerator to support. Contract prices are still rising ( over 3 months), far below the peak. It keeps margins high but will add less growth from here.
  3. HBM is taking over as the growth driver. The HBM proxy is re-pricing upward: → per gram YoY, consistent with the HBM4 shift. TrendForce expects blended HBM prices to rise a further 121% in 2027. HBM is where SK hynix leads.
  4. New capacity is being absorbed. M15X is built and ramping, and prices kept rising through the ramp. So far, prices show no sign of oversupply.

Outlook: constructive into 2027. Growth should slow from 2026's pace as commodity price gains fade, but stay positive as the mix shifts toward HBM, SK hynix's strongest product, while new supply is absorbed.

How the numbers are made

  • Unit value = value ÷ weight, with value and weight summed over the period first. Monthly averages of unit value are never used.
  • YoY split: ln(V₁/V₀) = ln(W₁/W₀) + ln(UV₁/UV₀). Exact by construction.
  • Momentum = level vs 3 months earlier. Missing months stay blank; nothing is filled.
  • The quarter-to-date forecast has no fitted parameters. It uses last quarter's revenue and the export ratio directly, and its form was picked on the same 18 quarters it is tested on. Code: src/analysis/quarter_to_date_forecast.py. Site data: src/site/export_site_data.py, which re-checks its figures against the analysis outputs.

Limitations

  • HS 854232 includes Samsung and every other Korean memory exporter. It is a market signal, not an SK hynix signal.
  • Unit value is not a price. Product mix (HBM, high-capacity modules) moves it too.
  • DDR4 8Gb is a legacy part. It shows the commodity market, not HBM or server DDR5 contracts.
  • Small sample. backtest quarters. Direction is called well; the level can miss by up to .
  • Customs figures are today's revised values, so the backtest tests the method on revised data rather than live releases.
  • SK hynix's China fabs ship from China and are not in Korean customs data.
  • revenue is preliminary. The two financial files differ by on that quarter; the site uses the company figure in data/financials/skhynix_quarterly.csv, as the forecast does.
  • Satellite imagery at 10 m shows the building, not tools or wafer output. Index-based metrics (NDVI, NDBI, brightness) were too noisy and are not shown or claimed.
  • LLM use. An LLM read the press articles and extracted the dated facts (prices, quotes, construction milestones). Each row keeps its source link and quote, and no number is generated by the LLM. The NAND rows have not been checked by hand yet.
