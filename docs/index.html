Now. The shell was finished by about mid-2025; prices kept rising through the ramp.

Question

An upcycle ends when capacity catches up. Is M15X's capacity still to come, or already here?

What the images show

2022 to 2024: mixed texture inside the footprint, consistent with site works and a structure going up.

From 2025Q2: a uniform bright roof fills the footprint and matches the finished M15 roof beside it.

This fits the reported timeline: groundbreaking Oct 2022, cleanroom opened Oct 2025, 1b DRAM for HBM planned for early 2026.

Why it matters

InterpretationThe shell was finished by about mid-2025, so M15X supply is ramping in 2026, not still to come. The DDR4 8Gb contract price rose from () to () through that ramp. So far, demand has absorbed M15X's new output.

Caveat

10 m imagery shows the building, not tools, cleanroom fit-out, yields or wafer starts. Vegetation, built-up and brightness indices were tried and were too noisy (the site is pixels); no capacity measure is claimed from them. Yongin is not covered yet. The price evidence is DDR4 8Gb, a commodity proxy; M15X output is HBM-oriented 1b DRAM.

Not verifiedA large area north-west of M15 was cleared in 2023–24 and shows new structures in 2025–26. It may be the packaging fab confirmed for Cheongju in Jan 2026.

How the numbers are made

  • Unit value = value ÷ weight, with value and weight summed over the period first. Monthly averages of unit value are never used.
  • YoY split: ln(V₁/V₀) = ln(W₁/W₀) + ln(UV₁/UV₀). Exact by construction.
  • Momentum = level vs 3 months earlier. Missing months stay blank; nothing is filled.
  • The quarter-to-date forecast has no fitted parameters. Code: src/analysis/quarter_to_date_forecast.py. Site data: src/site/export_site_data.py, which re-checks its figures against the analysis outputs.

Limitations

  • HS 854232 includes Samsung and every other Korean memory exporter. It is a market signal, not an SK hynix signal.
  • Unit value is not a price. Product mix (HBM, high-capacity modules) moves it as much as pricing does.
  • DDR4 8Gb is a legacy part. It shows the commodity market, not HBM or server DDR5 contracts.
  • Small sample. backtest quarters. Direction is called well; the level can miss by up to .
  • Customs figures are current revised values, so the backtest is not a true real-time test.
  • SK hynix's China fabs ship from China and are not in Korean customs data.
  • revenue is preliminary. The two financial files differ by on that quarter; the site uses the company figure in data/financials/skhynix_quarterly.csv, as the forecast does.
  • Satellite imagery at 10 m shows the building, not tools or wafer output. Index-based metrics (NDVI, NDBI, brightness) were too noisy and are not shown or claimed.
  • LLM use. Used only to extract dated facts (prices, quotes, construction milestones) from source documents. A person approved every extracted row. No LLM-generated numbers.
